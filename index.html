History · gardens

Imperial Palace East Gardens & grounds

Explore the public East Gardens and surrounding palace grounds near Tokyo Station. The East Gardens have scheduled closures and may be closed during the New Year holiday; check official opening dates before visiting. The inner palace is not generally open for casual visits.
